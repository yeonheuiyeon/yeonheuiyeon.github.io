How to draft an ancillary-sales planning memo

List each planning candidate (service candidate, bundle option, review question) with the published sales statistic it relies on, the sample period, and the condition it assumes. Compare service candidates against published attach-rate bands only, stating the band and period used. Where a digest is silent on a sales condition, mark the item as unverified rather than filling it in. The carrier's ancillary attach-rate target for the new service is compared by the ancillary desk in its internal memo; public pages carry only public material. Actual fare changes and sales launches are performed by the carrier's own staff.

What cannot be verified from public pages alone

  • Whether a sales digest reflects a later bundle revision
  • Whether a comparable-case table omits a route-seasonality constraint
  • Whether a guidance note hides a channel-pricing limit
