How to draft an expansion launch planning memo

List each capacity and invite-wave candidate with the published case or statistic it relies on, the sample period, and the condition it assumes. Compare candidate server capacity against published concurrency guidance only, stating the band and period used. Where a public case is silent on an operations condition, mark the item as unverified rather than filling it in. The studio's own closed-beta invite count is compared by the live-operations team in its internal memo; public pages carry only public material.
