How to draft a season settlement planning memo

List each settlement band candidate with the public source and statistic it relies on, and the sample period used. Compare band candidates using only the public figures of the two sources, stating the source and period for each. Where a source is silent on a segment condition, mark the item as unverified rather than filling it in. The desk's target claim-settlement reserve for the season is a figure the settlement desk keeps for internal planning only; this public guide carries public material only.
