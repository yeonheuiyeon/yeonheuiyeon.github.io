How to draft the scenario memo

List each loss-assumption and capital candidate with the published scenario it relies on. Compare across scenarios only on the axes the tables publish. Where a listing omits a funding horizon, mark the item as unverified rather than extrapolating it. The memo is internal and does not commit the bank to any filing.
