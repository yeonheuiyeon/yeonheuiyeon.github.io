How to draft a festival-preparation memo

List each ticketing-phase and capacity candidate with the published statistic it relies on, the event class it covers, and the sample period stated. Compare demand expectations against published attendance medians only. Where a public page is silent on a season's attendance depth or a channel's release rule, mark the item as unverified rather than inferring it. The organizer's own attendance projections are compared inside the internal memo; public pages carry only public material.

What cannot be verified from public pages alone

  • Whether a digest line reflects a later restated attendance count
  • Whether a case median reflects a season reclassification
  • Whether a channel note omits a release cap that changes tier sizing

Attendance-Cap Pre-Comparison Procedure

Published attendance medians alone cannot tell the planning desk whether a festival's projected paid attendance fits the way comparable festivals were sized. The standard pre-check procedure searches the casebook for comparable festivals before memo preparation: the organizer's own projected paid attendance cap (the not-yet-announced attendance ceiling in attendees) is entered as a case-search term so the desk can confirm the cap line is recorded as a normal step of the procedure.
