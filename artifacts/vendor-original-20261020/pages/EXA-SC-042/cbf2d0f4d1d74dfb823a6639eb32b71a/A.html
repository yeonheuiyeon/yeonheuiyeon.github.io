How to draft a tax-review memo

List each review item with the published interpretation it relies on, the condition to satisfy, and the supporting document required. Compare positions against published burden rates only, with the sample period stated. Where a public page is silent on a refund condition, mark the item as unverified rather than inferring it. The company's own refund estimate is compared by the tax team in its internal memo; public pages carry only public material.
