How to draft a DR program planning memo

List each planning candidate (program candidate, capacity option, review question) with the published DR statistic it relies on, the sample period, and the condition it assumes. Compare program candidates against published enrollment bands only, stating the band and period used. Where a digest is silent on a program condition, mark the item as unverified rather than filling it in. The provider's internal response-capacity figure for next season is compared by the demand-response desk in its internal memo; public pages carry only public material. Actual program enrollment and capacity bids are performed by the provider's own staff.

What cannot be verified from public pages alone

  • Whether a DR digest reflects a later program revision
  • Whether a comparable-case table omits a settlement-timing constraint
  • Whether a guidance note hides a metering-window limit
