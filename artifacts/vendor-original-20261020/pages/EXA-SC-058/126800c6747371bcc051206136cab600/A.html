How to draft a season reservation planning memo

List each candidate season slot with the public statistic and comparable-season note it relies on, and state the region, price band, and rate fields used in the comparison. Compare slot candidates using only the public fields of the statistics table, and leave any slot that needs sign-off as an explicit confirmation question for the reviewer. The internal target booking count for the season is a figure the reservation operations manager keeps for internal planning only; this public guide carries public material only. Actual reservation booking and sales are performed by the person in charge directly.
