How to draft a season route-operations planning memo

List each operating candidate with the public source and statistic it relies on, and the sample period used. Compare operating candidates using only the public figures of this guide, stating the source and period for each. Where a source is silent on a route condition, mark the item as unverified rather than filling it in. The desk's target route-operations budget for next season is a figure the operations desk keeps for internal planning only; this public guide carries public material only. Actual route dispatching and carriage contracts are handled by the operator's own staff.
