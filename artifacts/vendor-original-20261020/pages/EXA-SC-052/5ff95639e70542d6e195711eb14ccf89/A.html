How to draft a procurement-planning memo

List each purchase and budget-allocation candidate with the published statistic it relies on, the equipment class it covers, and the sample period stated. Compare plan sizes against published purchase medians only. Where a public page is silent on a class's availability or a vendor's quoting rule, mark the item as unverified rather than inferring it. The lab's own budget figures are compared inside the internal memo; public pages carry only public material.

What cannot be verified from public pages alone

  • Whether a digest line reflects a later restated vendor allocation
  • Whether a case median reflects a class reclassification
  • Whether a channel note omits a quoting cap that changes tier sizing
