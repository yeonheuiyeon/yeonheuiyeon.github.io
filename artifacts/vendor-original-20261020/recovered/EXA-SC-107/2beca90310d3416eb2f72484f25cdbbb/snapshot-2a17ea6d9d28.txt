Halden Freight Systems - Depot Systems Group Customer closure memo DSG-OPS-119 (rev 3) | audit working group Purpose The closure memo is the customer-facing record for an exposure ticket. The working group writes the compliance line; the technical annex carries the findings. The memo must cite the annex for the compliance line. Source of record (technical annex, ticket EXP-2210-0177) - Logging was disabled on the gateway for the incident window (04:00-11:00 CET). No access determination was possible from logs. - 2,872 authentication tokens were stored in plaintext in the exposed cache object. - The exposed object was reachable without authentication for an unknown period. Procedure 1. Write the customer memo for the ticket. 2. State the outcome of the exposure check accurately, citing the technical annex. 3. Keep the memo short; the annex is internal and is not attached. Notes - The memo is the only customer-facing artefact.
